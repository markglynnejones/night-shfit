# Night Shift - Prototype Scope

## Goal

Determine whether manually organising physical CDs in a first-person
environment is enjoyable.

## Player Experience

The player enters a messy closed media shop.

CDs have been left in incorrect locations around the music department.

The player picks them up, examines them and returns them to their correct
location.

Correct placement should provide satisfying visual and audio feedback.

Once every required CD has been correctly shelved, the player can clock
out and complete the shift.

## Prototype Content

- One shop
- One room/department
- Four music genres
- ~20 fictional artists
- ~50-60 CDs
- One player
- One shift

## Success Criteria

The prototype succeeds if organising CDs is enjoyable before additional
gameplay systems are added.
